Break Free Studio

Short videos about hidden meanings in symbols, myths and scripture.

Break Free Studio is the private publishing tool behind the Higher Knowledge short-video channel. It is used by one creator to publish the channel's own videos to TikTok and YouTube and to see how those videos perform.

What it does

When a new video is ready, the creator reviews it and approves it. Only then does the tool upload that video to the creator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. Each day it reads the public numbers for the channel's own videos (views, likes, comments and shares) so the creator can learn which topics people enjoy.

The tool is not offered to the public. It never posts without the creator's approval, never posts to anyone else's account, and never reads anyone else's data.

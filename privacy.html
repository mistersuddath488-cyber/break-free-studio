Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Break Free Studio (the "tool") is a private publishing tool used by one creator to post their own videos to TikTok and YouTube and to track how those videos perform. This policy explains what it collects and why.

What we collect

Only from the creator's own connected accounts:

  • basic profile information (display name, profile picture, follower count);
  • the list of the creator's own videos and their public numbers (views, likes, comments, shares, and on YouTube, watch time and subscribers gained);
  • the access tokens TikTok and Google issue after the creator signs in, so the tool can post approved videos.

The tool does not collect information about viewers, followers or any other TikTok or YouTube users.

How it is used

To upload videos the creator has approved, and to show the creator which videos perform best. Nothing else.

Where it is stored

On the creator's own computer. It is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and it is not used for advertising.

How long

Performance numbers are kept while the channel is active. Access tokens are deleted when the creator disconnects the tool or asks for deletion.

Deleting your data

Disconnect the tool on TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions), or email us and everything stored for your account is deleted within 30 days.

Children

The tool is not meant for anyone under 18.
